# The Writer's Path
{: .fs-8 }

**Goal: become a professional writer.**
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

**Status:** In progress — **1/15 complete** · **Started:** 2026-07-25 · **15 modules, 4 stages, 4 milestones**

---

## What "professional" means here

Not a vibe — a standard. By the end of this course you will have:

1. **A portfolio of three published pieces** in three different modes (a crafted essay, a reported story, a piece in your own established voice).
2. **A pitch accepted by an outlet you don't own.** Anyone can publish themselves; a professional gets past an editor.
3. **A voice you can describe** — and reproduce on demand, under deadline.
4. **A working vocabulary** you actually deploy, built by system rather than by accident.

Everything below is scaffolding toward those four things. No module exists that doesn't feed them.

## The ladder

Each stage is a rung. You can't skip one, because each stage's milestone is the raw material for the next.

**Stage I — The Sentence.** You cannot write a good essay out of bad sentences. Everything starts at the level of the line.
→ *Milestone 1: a 600-word piece, ruthlessly revised — clean prose, zero clutter.*

**Stage II — Voice & Style.** Competent prose is invisible; professional prose is *someone's*. This stage is how you get from "correct" to "unmistakably yours."
→ *Milestone 2: one story told in three borrowed voices, then once in your own.*

**Stage III — Forms That Pay.** Style without structure is decoration. Here you learn the architectures professionals actually get hired to build.
→ *Milestone 3: a fully reported piece with a real interview and a real structure.*

**Stage IV — The Professional.** The craft that surrounds the craft: speaking, the law, the pitch, the business.
→ *Capstone: portfolio of three published pieces + one accepted pitch.*

## How this course differs from the others

Writing is creative, so the teaching comes from **humans who have done it** — Orwell, Didion, Vonnegut, Baldwin, Talese, Zinsser, Saunders, Lamott, McPhee — through their own essays, interviews, and recorded talks. My job is to sequence them, tell you what to watch for, question you hard, and edit your work honestly.

The other difference: **every module ends in words on a page.** Knowledge modules can be passed by understanding. This one can only be passed by producing.
