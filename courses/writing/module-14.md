# Module 14: Media Law & Ethics

**Stage IV — The Professional** · **Status:** Not started

**Core question:** What can you actually publish without being sued or disgraced?

**What you walk away with:** Working knowledge of libel, fair use, privacy, and corrections — the floor a professional cannot fall below.

## Watch & Read

The anchor sources for this module. Understanding checks and written questions are added when the module opens.

**Read: [Defamation — Digital Media Law Project](https://www.dmlp.org/legal-guide/defamation)**

> ~25 min. Harvard's Berkman Center guide, written for independent publishers. Covers the elements of a defamation claim, the actual-malice standard for public figures, and the defenses. This is the single most important legal reading of your career.

**Read: [Risks Associated with Publication — DMLP](https://www.dmlp.org/legal-guide/risks-associated-publication)**

> ~20 min. Privacy, right of publicity, and the rest of the exposure surface.

**Read: [SPJ Code of Ethics](https://www.spj.org/ethicscode.asp)**

> ~10 min. Short, and the standard American newsrooms are held to.
