# Module 12: Journalism: Reporting & the Lede

**Stage III — Forms That Pay** · **Status:** Not started

**Core question:** How do professionals find stories, get people to talk, and open hard?

**What you walk away with:** The reporting process end to end: story-finding, source development, interviewing, and the lede that earns the next paragraph.
