# Module 15: The Professional Writer

**Stage IV — The Professional** · **Status:** Not started

**Core question:** How do you get paid to do this?

**What you walk away with:** Pitching, placing, and building a beat — the business layer that turns skill into a profession.

_Delivered when you reach this module — the professor's briefing on what these sources are doing and what to watch for._

## Watch & Read

The anchor sources for this module. Understanding checks and written questions are added when the module opens.

**Read: [The Open Notebook — how to pitch](https://www.theopennotebook.com/pitch-database/)**

> ~30 min. A database of real accepted pitches with the editors' commentary — the most useful free resource in freelance journalism.

**Read: [Study Hall / freelance resources](https://poynter.org/business-work/)**

> ~15 min. Rates, contracts, and the working economics.

**Read: [Paris Review interviews — any three writers on the working life](https://www.theparisreview.org/interviews)**

> ~45 min. Read three interviews looking only for how they organized their working days and handled money.
