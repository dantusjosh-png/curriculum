# Module 5: What Style Actually Is

**Stage II — Voice & Style** · **Status:** Not started

**Core question:** Is style something you have, or something you do?

**What you walk away with:** The ability to read any writer's style as a set of specific, repeatable, stealable choices.

_Delivered when you reach this module — the professor's briefing on what these sources are doing and what to watch for._

## Watch & Read

The anchor sources for this module. Understanding checks and written questions are added when the module opens.

**Read: [Kurt Vonnegut Explains How to Write With Style](https://www.openculture.com/2014/11/kurt-vonnegut-explains-how-to-write-with-style.html)**

> ~10 min. Vonnegut's eight rules, written for an International Paper ad campaign in 1980 and better than most books on the subject. Watch for rule one — find a subject you care about — and for what he says about sounding like where you came from.

**Read: [Ernest Hemingway, The Art of Fiction No. 21 — The Paris Review](https://www.theparisreview.org/interviews/4825/the-art-of-fiction-no-21-ernest-hemingway)**

> ~30 min. The most famous writing interview ever conducted. Hemingway on the iceberg theory, on stopping while you still know what happens next, on rewriting the ending of *A Farewell to Arms* thirty-nine times.

## Application

A style autopsy: take 200 words from a writer you admire and identify ten specific mechanical choices — sentence length pattern, verb density, adjective count, punctuation habits, register — that produce the effect.
