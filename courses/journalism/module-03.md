# Module 3: Verification & Ethics

**Status:** Not started

**Core question:** What you can trust, what you can print, and what you owe
your subject and your reader.

**Anchor resources (verified live):**
- [Verification Fundamentals: Rules to Live By](https://datajournalism.com/read/handbook/verification-1/verification-fundamentals-rules-to-live-by/2-verification-fundamentals-rules-to-live-by) — Steve Buttry, in the *Verification Handbook*. The core questions: "How do you know that?" and "How else do you know that?" — plus why even honest witnesses and authoritative sources need independent verification, not just trust.
- [SPJ Code of Ethics](https://www.spj.org/spj-code-of-ethics/) — the official Society of Professional Journalists code: seek truth and report it, minimize harm, act independently, be accountable and transparent.
