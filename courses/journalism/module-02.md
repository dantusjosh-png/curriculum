# Module 2: The Interview

**Status:** Not started

**Core question:** Getting someone to tell you something true — question
structure, listening, silence, and the judgment calls that don't show up in a
checklist.

**Anchor resource (verified live):** [An Illustrated Guide to the Basics of
Interviewing](https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2023/illustrated-guide-interviewing-basics-journalism/)
— Poynter, 2023, based on Roy Peter Clark's 2015 piece (nice continuity: same
author as Module 1, and as the "Fifty Writing Tools" reading in Writer's Path
Module 3). Covers one-question-at-a-time, open-ended questions, sitting in
silence rather than filling it, note-taking vs. recording, how closely to
stick to a prepared list, and reading a source's mood.
