# Journalism

**Status:** In progress — Module 1 · **Started:** 2026-07-31

The practice side of the craft: not how to write a good sentence (that's
[The Writer's Path](/courses/writing/)), but how to find a story, get someone
to tell you something true, know what you can trust and what you owe your
subject, and get the finished piece into an editor's hands. Four modules,
compressed — reporting fundamentals first, then the business of actually
getting published.

## Syllabus

| # | Module | Core question | Status |
|:--|:-------|:--------------|:-------|

## Prerequisites & positioning

No prerequisites, though it pairs naturally with **The Writer's Path** —
that course is the craft of the sentence and the page; this one is the craft
of the reporting that fills them. Aimed at someone who wants to actually
publish freelance journalism, not just study it: every module's application
exercise asks you to do the real version of the skill (a real potential
source, a real pitch), not a hypothetical one.
