# How It Works

Each course here is a condensed degree-level program delivered in an interactive, dialogue-driven style — rigor, structure, and progressive depth, compressed into a short course.

## Pedagogical framework

- **Language** — clear, concise, academically rigorous, still accessible.
- **Interactivity** — constant engagement: probing, Socratic-style questions that adapt to answers.
- **Depth** — the authority of a full degree program, compressed.
- **Real-world integration** — analogies, case studies, applied examples anchor every abstraction.
- **Progression** — fundamentals first, then systematic advancement to complex, specialized knowledge.

## Structure of every course

### 1. Syllabus
Modules ordered from basics to advanced, modeled on a degree curriculum.

### 2. Each module
- **Lecture** — concise but rich: theory, context, examples.
- **Discussion** — conceptual and applied comprehension questions.
- **Application** — one short exercise, case study, or thought experiment.
- **Feedback loop** — readiness check; struggling triggers a reteach with fresh examples.

### 3. Section reviews
Mini-quizzes, summaries, and synthesis prompts between sections.

### 4. Capstone & reflection
A final integrative project simulating real-world application of everything learned, followed by reflection: *how would you apply this in your field or daily life?*

## How the site is organized

- Every course lives in the **sidebar** as a top-level section.
- Each module is a **child page** under its course — lecture notes, exercises, and your work get recorded there as we go.
- The **Home** page tracks the catalog and per-course progress.
- **Search** (top bar) works across every course.
